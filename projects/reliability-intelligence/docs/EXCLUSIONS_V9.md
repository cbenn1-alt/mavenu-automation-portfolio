**Data handling:** Fixture identifiers start SYN. Do not replace these values with real case IDs, personally identifying information, credentials, trade-secret plant layouts, field measurements or client data. The automation should never contact customer systems.

**Fallback or no-build:** The concept may be discontinued or redirected to approved incumbent CMMS/ERP/Power BI functionality if business discovery shows a sufficient existing solution. Buyer, user, cohort and proposed 20% review-time target have not been validated.

**Acceptance is explicitly conditional:** Passing the automated clean-room replay proves only the documented local synthetic behavior. Independent human testing and formal owner approval must occur before claiming the V9 proof requirement is fully met.
